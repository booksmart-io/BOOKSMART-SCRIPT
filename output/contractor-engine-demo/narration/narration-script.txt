This is BookSmart's contractor financial intelligence engine. Its job is not to create another bookkeeping dashboard. It brings together trusted accounting activity, bank movement, Jobber operations, receipts, and monitoring logic, then tells the contractor what changed, what looks risky, and what needs action.
The experience begins with a morning brief. BookSmart uses canonical approved accounting records for revenue, expenses, net income, and cash movement. It highlights connection problems, active tasks, and the highest-priority findings. Notice that when a required balance or obligation is missing, BookSmart says more information is needed instead of inventing an answer.
The Money view demonstrates the source-of-truth rule. QuickBooks-backed or approved BookSmart accounting data remains canonical. Plaid verifies cash movement and balances, while Jobber provides operational context. If the same payment appears in Jobber, QuickBooks, and the bank, it is counted once. Here we can see recognized spending and net cash movement, with unsupported balances clearly withheld.
The engine turns supported signals into persistent tasks. Each task has an owner, priority, due date, and a deterministic lifecycle, so the same issue does not create duplicates every day. In this example, BookSmart identifies disconnected financial sources, a Jobber job that needs invoicing, unscheduled work, negative cash movement, and a material expense change.
Insights explain why each alert exists. The contractor can see the condition, the current evidence, the comparison period, the recommended next step, and whether CPA review is appropriate. Operational Jobber information never becomes accounting revenue by itself. The system preserves provenance so the user can trace an insight back to the supporting connection, transaction, job, invoice, receipt, or document.
When professional review is justified, BookSmart routes the issue to My CPA. Access is controlled by the engagement status and owner permissions. The CPA sees canonical summaries, approved supporting activity, and tasks specifically assigned or marked for review. BookSmart does not copy the data into a second ledger, and preview-only Jobber findings remain private until the owner authorizes sharing.
That is the contractor engine today: one financial source of truth, operational job context, explainable signals, concrete tasks, and controlled CPA escalation. The core implementation is about ninety percent complete, with overall production readiness around eighty-two percent. The remaining work is primarily production validation, deeper provider coverage, and advanced supplier and historical job analytics.
